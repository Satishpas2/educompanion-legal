EduCompanion — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 August 2026 Applies to: the EduCompanion Android app (com.educompanion.app)

A lawyer should review this against the DPDP Act before the app is listed. It is accurate about what the software does, which is necessary for that review but not a substitute for it — in particular, the Act's requirement for verifiable parental consent is stricter than the confirmation this app currently records.

In short

EduCompanion is a learning app for children in classes 3 to 8. It teaches a chapter out loud and listens to the child's spoken answers.

  • We record your child's voice when they answer a question, and send the recording to our server and to OpenAI to turn it into text.
  • Photos of textbook pages never leave the phone. They are read on the device itself; only the recognised words and any cropped diagrams are sent.
  • There is no advertising, no tracking across other apps, and nothing is ever sold.
  • You can delete everything at any time, from inside the app or by emailing us.

Parental consent

The app is intended for children, so it does not collect anything until a parent or guardian has read the disclosure shown when the app first opens and agreed to it. Until that happens the app makes no network requests at all.

You can withdraw consent at any time by deleting your data (below) or by uninstalling the app.

What we collect, and why

What Why Where it goes
Recordings of your child's voice To turn a spoken answer into text so it can be marked Our server, and OpenAI for transcription
The text of your child's answers To mark the answer and decide what to teach next Our server, and OpenAI

What we deliberately do not collect

  • Photographs of textbook pages — these are read on the phone and stay there.
  • Your child's date of birth, gender, address, or school.
  • Location of any kind.
  • Contacts, calendar, photos library, or any other app's data.
  • Advertising identifiers. The app does not contain an advertising SDK and does not use the Android Advertising ID.
  • Device identifiers such as IMEI, MAC address, or Android ID. The app previously used the Android ID and no longer does; the identifier it uses now is randomly generated and is destroyed when the app is uninstalled.

Who else sees the data

We share data with these processors only, and only for the purposes above:

  • OpenAI — transcribes speech, writes the lesson text, and marks answers. Data sent to OpenAI's API is not used to train their models.
  • Google Firebase — Authentication (sign-in) and Remote Config (the server address). Firebase Analytics is not used and no analytics or advertising SDK is present in the app.
  • Microsoft Azure — runs our server and stores the data described above.

We do not sell personal information, and we do not share it for anyone else's advertising or marketing.

Where data is stored

Our servers are in Central India, so your child's lessons stay in India. OpenAI processes the speech and lesson text in the United States. By using the app you consent to that transfer.

How long we keep it

  • Lesson history, chapters and recordings: until you delete them, or until the account has been inactive for 24 months.
  • Reports you send about something the tutor said: 12 months. These hold what the tutor said, not what your child said.
  • Server logs (times and error codes, no lesson content): 30 days.
  • Database backups: 7 days, after which a deleted account is gone from those too.
  • Account records: until the account is deleted.

Deleting your data

In the app: Settings → Account → Delete my data. This removes every chapter, lesson, conversation and recording from our servers for every child on the account, and deletes the account itself. It happens immediately and cannot be undone.

By email: write to contact@artinsat.com from the address on the account, or describe the account if there isn't one, and we will delete it within 30 days.

See also the standalone deletion page: https://satishpas2.github.io/educompanion-legal/delete

AI-generated content

The explanations, questions and feedback in this app are generated by a language model. It is usually accurate and is sometimes wrong. Please look at what your child is being taught.

Every tutor message has a Report button. Reports reach a person, and we read all of them.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to see the data we hold, to correct it, to have it deleted, to restrict how it is used, and to complain to a regulator. Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023 you may also nominate someone to exercise these rights on your behalf.

To exercise any of these, write to contact@artinsat.com.

Security

The app refuses to send anything over an unencrypted connection: every request is HTTPS, enforced by the app itself rather than left to the network.

Every request must carry a signed token proving which account it belongs to, and each account can reach only its own data — a request naming someone else's account is refused whatever it claims.

No system is perfectly secure. If a breach affects your child's information we will tell you and the relevant regulator.

Changes

If we change what the app collects, we will ask for consent again inside the app rather than relying on this page having been updated.
