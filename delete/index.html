Deleting your EduCompanion data

Published at https://satishpas2.github.io/educompanion-legal/delete. Enter that URL in the Play Console under App content → Data safety → Data deletion. Google requires a deletion route that works for someone who has already uninstalled the app, which is why this page exists separately from the in-app button.

EduCompanion is used by children, and you can remove everything it holds about your child at any time. There is no waiting period and nothing is kept back.

Option 1 — in the app (immediate)

  1. Open EduCompanion.
  2. Tap the ⚙ settings icon at the top left.
  3. Answer the multiplication question (this is there so a child cannot do this by accident).
  4. Tap Account.
  5. Tap Delete my data, then confirm.

This deletes, for every child on the account:

  • every chapter you added, including the text read from the pages and any diagrams;
  • every lesson and the full conversation, including what your child said;
  • the diagrams held on the phone;
  • the account itself.

The app carries on working afterwards, as if newly installed.

Option 2 — by email

If you have uninstalled the app, or cannot get to the button, write to:

contact@artinsat.com

Send it from the email address on the account. If you never made an account, tell us roughly when the app was used and the child's first name as entered, and we will find and delete the records.

We will confirm within 30 days.

What is deleted, and what is not

Deleted immediately:

Data Where it lived
Chapters, page text, diagrams Our server
Lessons, questions, answers, progress Our server
The diagrams cropped from your textbook pages Your device
Your account and sign-in details Firebase Authentication
Everything the app stored on the phone Your device

Kept, and why:

Data Why For how long
Reports you sent about something the tutor said To fix the problem for other children. These hold the tutor's words, not your child's. 12 months
Basic server logs (times and error codes, no lesson content) Security and diagnosing faults 30 days
Database backups Recovering from failure. A deleted account disappears from these as they age out. 7 days

No image and no recording is ever uploaded — textbook photos, the diagrams cropped from them, and your child's voice are all handled on the phone — so there is nothing of that kind on our servers to delete. Removing the app removes them from the device.
